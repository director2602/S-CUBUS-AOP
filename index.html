Methodology

Assumptions & Notes

  1. Student mix totals the target above and can be changed without changing the model logic.
  2. School segment (Class 8–10) and the JEE/NEET + repeater segment each have their own ARPU; blended ARPU is the revenue-weighted average of whatever mix is entered.
  3. All listed salaries/packages receive the hike % above for FY27.
  4. Marketing budget is set as a % of revenue; target blended CAC is ≤ ₹12,000.
  5. Rent, electricity and printing/machines/other are entered monthly and annualized (×12).
  6. EBITDA is before depreciation, interest, income tax, GST effects, scholarships/fee waivers, payment gateway charges and any faculty incentive/variable pay not entered here.
  7. Monthly revenue is a planning recognition model (average of opening and closing cumulative students × blended ARPU ÷ 12), not a cash-collection schedule. For cash control, track billed, collected and outstanding separately — see the Fee Portal.
  8. Recommended management rule: do not add headcount unless enrolments / capacity / batch size justify it.
  9. Recommended operating cadence: weekly admissions dashboard; monthly P&L + cash review; quarterly pricing and CAC review.
  10. Student-linked costs: material + T-shirt + bag + stationery, all editable above; stationery is a planning assumption because no exact figure was given.
  11. Every total on this page (P&L, break-even, KPIs, monthly plan) is derived from the same inputs in one calculation, so they cannot drift out of sync with each other the way the uploaded workbook had.
